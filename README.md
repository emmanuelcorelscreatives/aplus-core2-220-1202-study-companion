# A+ Core 2: support decisions companion

Independent learner companion for **220-1202, V15**. Prepared November 25, 2026.

This is a practical review aid, not a question bank, answer key, recalled exam content, official manual or exam-pass promise. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CompTIA. It complements, rather than replaces, the official objectives and hands-on preparation.

## Use the files

1. Read [Decision and troubleshooting review](DECISION-REVIEW.md) to connect symptoms to evidence and safe next steps.
2. Use [Study workflow](STUDY-WORKFLOW.md) to turn uncertain decisions into a short, repeatable learning cycle.
3. Follow [Official references](OFFICIAL-REFERENCES.md) when checking an OS-specific behavior or current objective.

The companion emphasizes Operating Systems, Security, Software Troubleshooting and Operational Procedures. It does not reproduce CompTIA's performance-based tasks or establish practical competence merely by being read.

## Safety boundary

Use only systems you own or are authorized to administer. Observe first. Changes, restarts, scans, repair operations and remote access require the applicable approval and recovery plan. Use a disposable lab for experiments; do not obtain live malware for these activities. Never put passwords, recovery keys, personal data or employer evidence into a public study journal.

Product menus, supported editions and recovery paths vary. Official links are supplied for verification; their availability was not checked live for this offline companion. No vendor manuals are redistributed in this package.
